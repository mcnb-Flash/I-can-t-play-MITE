8. 详细描述 (Description / Overview) —— 粘贴到编辑器（先切 Source/HTML 模式或直接粘 MD）
------------------------------------------------------------
ICPM ("I Can't Play MITE") is a fan-made, fully re-implemented port of the classic hardcore mod **MITE (Minecraft Is Too Easy, by Avernite)** onto **Minecraft 1.21.11 / Fabric**. Package and internal names are rewritten to avoid conflict; gameplay values follow the original R196 source tables.

## Features
- **Realistic satiation & nutrition**: dual food meters from the R196 table drive eating, regen and starvation; caps scale with level.
- **Living livestock**: hunger / thirst / crowding determine animal wellness; neglect them and outputs drop. Spook contagion scatters herds; offspring inherit parents' wellness.
- **Weighty tools & mining**: R196 mining rules, corrected tiers, negative XP slows you down.
- **XP that bites back**: XP can go negative (down to -40) with real penalties. Metal anvil fuses enchantment books, smashes books onto gear and renames.
- **Shield-mounted tools**: craft tool + shield into one; right-click to block and halve incoming damage (consumes only tool durability).
- **8-tier workbench line**: flint workbench up through adamantite, recipes aligned with R196, craft quality costs XP.
- **Fire-based cooking**: burn raw meat on combustible blocks with a flint & steel; multi-piece roasting with a discrete cooking window.
- **Seasons, moon phases & crops**: fertility, plant disease and seasonal behavior.
- **19 custom enchantments + curses**: witch curses as multi-state effects, curse decay, silver gear mechanics, undead-affinity combat.
- **New monsters**: dire wolves, earth/fire elementals, hellhounds, gelatinous cubes, giant zombies, miner zombies, skeleton & spider variants.
- **Buckets that obey physics**: lava/water/milk/stone buckets emit only flowing source; shift-scroll preview; 100 XP to place a source block; scooping never consumes the source.
- **Bows, arrows & fishing rods**: 9 arrow tiers, bows with R196 draw speeds, faithful fishing rods.
- **Experienced (XP) level 200 cap**, coin economy, runestones, portals to the underworld, and much more.
